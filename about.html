About me

The person behind
the strategy.

Francheska Guerrero is a product marketing strategist who lives on a horse farm in South Florida. When she's not hanging with her horses, or going to Jiu Jitsu practice she's watching French New Wave films and reading 19th century books.

She spent 3+ years at Mochee Digital translating complex, technical products into stories that actually land for aquaculture brands, cultural institutions, hospitality groups, and everything in between. Her superpower is sitting at the intersection of data and narrative: fluent in both SQL and a well-crafted sentence.

Right now, she's looking to bring that lens to early-stage robotics and automation companies, organizations building genuinely hard things that the world hasn't figured out how to talk about yet. That's exactly the problem she loves solving.
