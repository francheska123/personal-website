About Me


      

        
        

          
“I make the boring and technical interesting for people.”

        

      


      
Strategy & Market Intelligence Analyst


      

        FRANCHESKA GUERRERO has always been the connector. Give her something
        complicated and she'll figure out what's really being asked, then explain it so people
        can make a clear call.
      

      

        That curiosity doesn't stay in one lane. She makes video stories about people's lives,
        runs a series on cultural icons, built a café game, and writes about product, strategy,
        and culture at Tica Linda. Off screen, she's run a half-marathon, trains Brazilian
        Jiu-Jitsu, rides horses, and is building a cyberdeck from scratch.
      

      

        Lately it's pointed at the business of media: how streaming deals get made, how creators
        turn into consumer brands, what Apple is actually getting out of MLS, and whether
        equestrian could have its F1 moment. She's looking for a full-time strategy or market intelligence role where that curiosity
        is the job.
